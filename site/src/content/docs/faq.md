title: FAQ
description: Short answers about what the app does, what it costs, and what it does not do yet.
---

## Is it free?

There is nothing to buy, no account to create, and no ads. It is a personal project, not a product on sale.

## Does it need to be installed?

Not yet — there is no public installer. If you have it, someone built a copy for you. See [Getting the app](/installation/).

## Which computers does it run on?

It is made for Windows and also works on macOS and Linux. Windows gets the extra polish: the window corners, the frame and the tray icon are tuned for it.

## Can I close the app while something downloads?

Yes. Closing the window leaves the app running next to the clock and the download keeps going. The icon's menu brings the window back.

## Will it carry on after a restart?

Yes. Every download keeps a note of how far it got, so after a reboot it resumes where it stopped — at worst a couple of seconds of work is fetched again.

## Can it download videos from streaming sites?

No. It saves files that have a direct link. Video sites usually hide the video behind a player, and putting those streams back together is not something this app does. If a page offers a direct video or sound file, the browser add-on will list it for you.

## Can I limit how fast it goes?

Yes, either for everything at once in **Options → Network**, or for a single download in its **Speed limiter** tab.

## How many downloads at a time?

As many as you like, but a share of the connection goes to each. The queue buttons at the top start and stop the whole list.

## Can I schedule downloads for later?

Not yet. **Downloads → Scheduler and queues** says it is coming soon rather than opening a screen that does not work.

## Does it collect anything about me?

No account, no analytics, no update check. The only things it reaches out to are the files you ask it to download, plus a small connection to the browser add-on that stays on your own computer.

## Where are my downloads kept?

Wherever you chose for that download — by default your usual Downloads folder. Removing an entry from the list never deletes the file.

## Can I use the pictures and fonts in my own project?

The app is not published under a licence yet, so please ask before reusing any of it.
