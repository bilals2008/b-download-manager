## 0.3.0 — 2 November 2026

### Find any setting

- The search box at the top of Settings finds any option by name, even when you are on another page.
- Choose a result and the right page opens with the setting pointed out.

### A look that's yours

- Pick Light, Dark, Cyberpunk, Volt or Soft Black, with a small preview of each before you choose.

### A browser that just works

- The browser extension connects to the app on its own, with no code to copy.
- The video download button follows your choice on pages that are already open.
- Start Queue runs downloads in your order, as many at a time as you choose.

### Video downloads

- Choose a picture quality, and the app joins the picture and sound into one finished file.

## 0.2.0 — 31 October 2026

### A toolbar that fits

- Pick one of five toolbar looks, or use a custom style shared as a file.
- Switch between large and small buttons and hide only the ones you never use.
- Move buttons around or add separators; each style remembers its own layout.

### Sharing made simple

- Tell a friend now opens the share window on your computer.
- The website has a new page where you can preview every toolbar style.

### A fresher look

- Category icons have a softer shine that sits well beside the new toolbar.

## 0.1.3 — 30 October 2026

### Your files, your folders

- Choose a save folder for each category, and every new download follows it.
- Turn the video download button in your browser on or off from Settings.
- File icons now match the types you actually download.

### Clearer notes

- Release notes read as plain sentences instead of developer shorthand.
- Removing a download also clears the files it left behind and closes its windows.

## 0.1.2 — 29 October 2026

- File icons now look like pages with a coloured band, just like your desktop.
- The website shows real screenshots of the app.
- You can now get a helpful tip when the app starts, and updates can be checked after the window opens.

## 0.1.0 — 27 October 2026

- The first release: fast downloads with several connections, pause and resume, categories, and a tray icon that keeps things running.
