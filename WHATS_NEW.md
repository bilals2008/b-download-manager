## 1.3.0 — 17 November 2026

### Torrents

- A new tab in the torrent window draws the pieces of a download: what is on disk, what is still missing and what is on its way.
- A magnet link with no trackers, or whose trackers cannot be reached, now finds its peers through the distributed peer table and downloads anyway.
- Torrents find peers on your own network too: local discovery is on by default, and you can turn it off in the Torrents settings.
- A new choice asks your router to open the torrent port (UPnP or NAT-PMP). It is off by default, and the settings page says what the router answered.
- Torrent trackers now use the proxy you set in Options; peer connections still go directly, and the settings say so.
- YouTube videos download straight from the page at the quality you pick there, with captions chosen next to it; the extra window no longer opens.
- A quality in the page's download panel starts with one click.

## 1.2.1 - 16 November 2026

### Torrents

- Magnet links and .torrent files download like any other file, with their progress shown in a torrent window.
- Add a torrent from a link, a file or an address, and pick the files you want before it starts.
- A finished torrent can keep sharing; choose the ratio or the minutes it stops at, and how fast it uploads.

## 1.2.0 - 15 November 2026

### Your words, your language

- The window reads in English, French, German and Arabic; Arabic reads right to left.

### Your queue on your schedule

- Tell the queue which days and hours it runs; it runs and stops by itself, and powers the computer off only when you say so.

### Videos come with captions

- Pick the captions a page offers, or save the sound of a video as MP3, Opus or FLAC.

### Google Drive in one click

- Drive files download as files, documents as PDF or Word, and image boards as a folder of pictures.

### A smarter network

- Names can be looked up through an encrypted resolver or NextDNS, downloads can be held to one network adapter, and every download shows its server and its round trip.

### Host links and your accounts

- Real-Debrid, AllDebrid and Premiumize unlock the links they take.

### Your phone, and your assistant

- A phone on your home network and an AI assistant will help next; both are coming soon.

### Small stuff

- The Add URL window starts downloads straight away; archives unpack after they finish, asking a password when needed; a frozen window is reported on the next start.

## 1.1.1 - 9 November 2026

- Video downloads now finish properly, with picture and sound in one file.
- Stop keeps what was already downloaded; Resume continues from there.
- The browser panel follows your app theme, and Facebook videos show HD and SD choices.

## 1.1.0 — 8 November 2026

### The browser extension adds more

- Videos and audio a page plays on its own now show up in the extension too, not just files you click.
- Grabbed media goes straight to your download list like everything else.

### YouTube downloads that finish

- A YouTube download now keeps trying on its own when the source acts up, and joins picture and sound before it calls itself done.
- Each track keeps the identity it was fetched with, so more videos download instead of failing.

### Files land where they belong

- A download that belongs to a category is saved in that category's own folder.

### A brighter Bloom

- The Bloom theme now comes in a light version, and the Appearance page shows a colour swatch for every theme.

## 1.0.0 — 6 November 2026

### A look that's yours

- Three new themes join the list: Graphite, Bloom and Hive.
- Each theme has its own token file, so switching between them is instant.

### Video in one file

- Choose a YouTube quality and the app saves the finished video with picture and sound already joined.
- No extra tools are needed to download or merge a video.

## 0.3.2 — 4 November 2026

### A quieter look

- The windows lost their heavy borders and the Settings pages were redrawn, so more of your list fits on screen.
- A new dark theme, Ayu Dark, is waiting for you in Appearance.

### Keys you choose

- Every action now has a keyboard shortcut, and you can change any of them in Settings.
- The menus and tooltips show the key that works, so nothing has to be looked up.

### Files where they belong

- A download that is not finished yet stays in the app's own storage; only the finished file lands in your download folder.
- A download that starts on one drive and finishes on another now works.

### Updates

- The installer wears the app's own mark and shows a real progress bar while the new version downloads.

### In the browser

- The video button appears as soon as the pointer is on a player, the panel stays open while you pick a quality, and the browser shows the quality names the page offers.
